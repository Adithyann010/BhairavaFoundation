About the Trust

Compassionate Community Care for Over Two Decades

The Bairava Charitable Trust is a registered non-profit organization dedicated to supporting senior citizens without family backing, providing hot nutritious daily meals, and offering student educational scholarships.

Our primary initiative, Daily Annadhanam, serves fresh nutritious meals to hundreds of elderly citizens, homeless individuals, and hospital attendants 365 days a year without pause. Our Senior Care Sanctuary provides full-time shelter, clothing, medical monitoring, and companionship to 40+ permanent senior residents.

The Trust operates with complete transparency, funded by the Bairava family and compassionate public patrons.

Trust Impact Metrics

500+ Meals Served Daily: Freshly prepared hot meals served at our Annadhanam center.
40+ Permanent Senior Residents: Safe housing, doctor care, nursing, and dignity in elder years.
150+ Annual Scholarships: Assisting underprivileged youth to complete schooling and college.

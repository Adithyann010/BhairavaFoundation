ABOUT SPORTS CLUB

HIGH-STANDARD INFRASTRUCTURE FOR EVERY ATHLETE

Bairava Sports Club is dedicated to building active, healthy communities by providing quality sports infrastructure, structured coaching programs, and regular competitive tournaments.

Whether you are an aspiring junior player looking for disciplined training, an amateur enthusiast booking evening court slots, or a corporate group organizing a sports tournament, our facilities provide a safe, modern, and energetic environment.

Led by certified coaches and experienced sports managers, we emphasize sportsmanship, fitness, and consistent athletic improvement.

WHY TRAIN AT BAIRAVA SPORTS CLUB

BWF-Standard Synthetic Courts: Shock-absorbent multi-court badminton arena with glare-free LED floodlights.
Structured Coaching Academies: Beginner to advanced training batches for children, youth, and adults.
Tournaments & League Hosting: Regular weekend tournaments, ranking matches, and corporate league events.

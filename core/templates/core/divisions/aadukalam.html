About Aadukalam

Reviving Indigenous Sports & Cultural Games

Bairava Aadukalam celebrates the rich sporting legacy of Tamil Nadu, creating vibrant platforms for grassroots athletes, rural sports practitioners, and community members of all generations.

From hosting district-level Kabaddi tournaments and traditional Silambam staff martial arts workshops to organizing community Kho-Kho, volleyball, and rural sports festivals, Aadukalam brings people together through the power of collective play and cultural celebration.

We nurture raw athletic talent with coaching guidance, tournament safety equipment, official refereeing, and awards that recognize hard work and sportsmanship.

Core Aadukalam Pillars

Preserving Heritage Sports: Dedicated focus on Kabaddi, Silambam, and traditional field games.
Grassroots Athletic Mentorship: Developing stamina, team coordination, and match discipline among rural and urban youth.
Community Gathering: Bringing families and spectators together in joyful, energetic sporting celebrations.

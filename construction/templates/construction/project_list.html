About Construction

End-to-End Civil & Architectural Excellence

With over two decades of hands-on civil engineering expertise in Chennai, Bairava Construction & Promoters carries residential, commercial, and property developments from initial architectural drawing to final handover.

We operate our own dedicated site teams, structural engineers, and skilled artisan craftsmen. From seismic-resistant RCC framing and foundation soil testing to customized false ceilings, modular kitchens, and bespoke interior elevations, every project is delivered with meticulous precision.

Our work is founded on transparent material specifications, strict compliance with municipal development guidelines, and predictable project milestones.

Our Construction Standards

Earthquake-Resistant RCC Framing: Structural integrity adhering to ISI structural specifications.
Transparent Estimation: Detailed bills of quantities without surprise cost escalations.
On-Time Turnkey Handover: Disciplined stage-wise site timelines and handover guarantees.
